HOW TO READ MISSING DATA

“Not observed” does not mean “does not exist.”

A device can block discovery, DNS may have no hostname, MAC addresses normally stay inside the local segment, and operating systems expose different Wi-Fi fields. Service verification checks only the selected device and 20 common TCP ports; it is not a complete 65,535-port scan.
